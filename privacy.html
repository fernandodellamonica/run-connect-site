Privacy Policy

This notice describes how Run Connect is designed to handle information in the Android application and on this project website.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Run Connect may process the following categories:

  • Account and authentication information: account email address and authentication identifiers used to sign in and associate cloud records with the correct account.
  • Running activity information: activity date and time, distance, duration, pace, speed, elevation, splits, activity identifiers, and related workout results.
  • Location information: GPS coordinates collected while a run is being recorded, plus a route representation derived from those coordinates for supported cloud-sync features.
  • Workout information: workout plans, steps, targets, and training settings that you create or use.
  • Synchronization information: record identifiers, sync status, timestamps, and deletion markers needed to synchronize changes and prevent deleted records from reappearing.

The exact device diagnostics, crash reports, and optional third-party software libraries included in each app release must be checked against that release before this notice is finalized.

3. Location, local storage, and route privacy

Run Connect is designed with a local-first approach. During activity recording, GPS trackpoints are stored locally on the Android device. Core run recording is designed to work without an internet connection.

When you sign in and enable cloud synchronization, selected activity information, splits, and workout information may be synchronized with the Run Connect backend. The current design keeps the raw GPS trackpoint list on the device. Where a route is synchronized, the app is designed to send a derived route representation after privacy-zone processing, rather than the original raw trackpoint list.

The app's geographic privacy processing is intended to suppress route points around configured sensitive areas, including activity start and finish areas. A route may consequently be shortened, split into segments, or omitted. Route masking reduces exposure but cannot guarantee that a person's location will never be inferred from other information.

Location access is used for recording running routes. Android permission controls can be used to manage location access; denying location access may prevent GPS activity recording from working as intended.

4. Cloud services and service providers

Run Connect uses Supabase for backend functions including authentication, database storage, and cloud synchronization. Supabase processes information on behalf of the project to provide those services.

The specific hosting region configured for the Run Connect Supabase project must be confirmed and stated here before publication. Supabase's own terms and privacy documentation also apply to its processing.

This project website is intended to be a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary technical information about website requests under its own privacy terms. The website does not currently include advertising tags, embedded analytics, contact forms, or third-party font imports.

5. Garmin and other integrations

Official Garmin Connect integration is not currently available in Run Connect. The app does not currently offer a live Garmin account connection. If an integration is implemented in the future, this policy will be updated before release to describe the information involved, the authorization process, and the relevant service providers.

6. Why information is processed

Information is processed to record and display running activities, calculate or present activity metrics, manage workouts, authenticate accounts, synchronize records, preserve data consistency, and maintain the security and operation of the app.

The specific legal basis applicable to each processing purpose must be confirmed as part of the final privacy review. Where a feature relies on a device permission or a consent choice, you can manage that permission or choice through the relevant app or Android controls, subject to the technical requirements of the feature.

7. When information is shared

Cloud information is shared with Supabase as needed to provide the backend services described above. Run Connect does not currently provide a public activity feed or public profile feature. No Garmin data exchange is currently active through the app.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to respond to a valid legal request or protect the security of users and the project.

8. Storage, retention, and deletion

Activity records and raw GPS trackpoints stored only on your device remain there until they are deleted in the app or the relevant device data is removed. Cloud-synchronized records are stored in the Run Connect backend while needed to provide account and synchronization features.

Synchronization may use deletion markers so that a record deleted on one device is not recreated by another device during a later sync. Such technical markers are not the same as the original activity content.

You may send a privacy or deletion request to runconnect@proton.me using the email address associated with your account. A request may need to be verified. Deleting cloud information does not automatically erase a separate copy that remains on your Android device.

The exact process for deleting an account and all associated cloud records, including any backup retention periods, must be verified and documented before general publication. No fixed deletion deadline is promised in this draft.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, you may request confirmation of processing, access to your personal information, correction of incomplete or inaccurate information, anonymization, blocking or deletion, information about sharing, and portability where applicable. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis.

To make a request, contact runconnect@proton.me. Please describe the request and identify the account concerned. The project may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request comes from the relevant person or their authorized representative.

10. Security

Run Connect uses account-based access controls and backend security policies intended to restrict access to cloud records. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information, but no device, network, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Family use and minors

Run Connect is currently a personal and family project, not a service designed specifically for children. A parent or legal guardian should supervise any use by a minor and assess whether the collection of account or location information is appropriate. This section must be reviewed against the actual intended age range before publication.

12. International processing

Because cloud infrastructure is provided by a third-party service provider, information may be stored or processed outside Brazil depending on the project's configured hosting region and the provider's systems. The relevant region and applicable transfer details must be confirmed before this notice is finalized.

13. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when app features, service providers, or data practices change. The last-reviewed date will be updated when a revised version is published.

14. Contact

For questions or requests about personal information, contact runconnect@proton.me.
