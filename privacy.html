Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Run Connect.

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Draft for review before publication

Publication checklist: Before publishing, the developer must confirm the legal operator name, production data flows, account-deletion procedure, and Supabase hosting region. Verify this draft against the released app.

1. Who operates Run Connect

Run Connect is an independent, non-commercial software project developed for personal and family use. It is not currently operated as a registered company or offered as a commercial subscription service.

Project contact: runconnect@proton.me.

Developer / data controller: [Insert developer's full legal name before publication], based in Brazil.

2. Information the app may handle

Depending on the features used, Run Connect may handle:

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers, display name, and preferences.
  • Running activities: date and time, activity type, distance, duration, pace, speed, elevation, splits, and device information where available.
  • Location and route information: GPS points collected during a recorded activity and route information derived from those points.
  • Workout information: workout plans, workout steps, targets, and training preferences.
  • Synchronization information: record identifiers, synchronization status, timestamps, and deletion markers.

3. Local storage and cloud synchronization

Run Connect is designed with a local-first approach. Activity recording and raw GPS trackpoint storage are performed on the device. Core run recording is designed to work without an internet connection.

If you sign in and use cloud synchronization, selected account, activity, split, and workout information may be sent to the Run Connect backend hosted by Supabase. Cloud synchronization is associated with the authenticated account.

Raw GPS trackpoints are intended to remain in local device storage. A cloud activity record may contain an encoded route representation after privacy-zone processing. The app is designed not to upload the raw trackpoint list.

4. Location privacy controls

Run Connect includes a privacy-zone mechanism intended to omit route points near configured sensitive locations, including start and end areas and intermediate passages through those areas. Depending on the route and settings, the route representation may be shortened, split into separate segments, or omitted.

Users should review privacy settings before enabling cloud synchronization. Route masking cannot guarantee that a person's location will never be inferred from other information.

5. Service providers

Run Connect uses Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database storage, and synchronization. Supabase processes information to provide those services and is subject to its own terms and privacy documentation.

Official Garmin Connect integration is being explored but is not currently available through Run Connect. If introduced, the app will request authorization before connecting a Garmin account and explain what information is exchanged. Garmin will process information under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. How information is used

Information is used to record and display activities, calculate or present running metrics, manage workouts, authenticate users, synchronize data, protect data integrity, and respond to support or privacy requests.

Run Connect is currently a personal, non-commercial project. It is not intended to sell personal information or use running data for third-party advertising.

7. Sharing

Information is not publicly displayed by Run Connect by default. It may be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed if required by applicable law or necessary to protect users and service security.

8. Retention and deletion

Local activity information remains on the device until deleted through the app or device storage is cleared. Cloud information is retained while needed to provide synchronization and maintain data integrity, subject to applicable deletion requests and technical backup-retention periods.

To request access, correction, or deletion of account-associated information, contact runconnect@proton.me from the email associated with the account. The developer may need to verify the request.

Deleting cloud information does not automatically erase copies remaining in local device storage. Local copies may need to be deleted separately.

9. Security

Run Connect uses account-based access controls and backend security policies intended to restrict access to each user's cloud records. If a future Garmin integration is approved and implemented, Garmin credentials are intended to be handled by secure backend components rather than embedded in the Android application.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should protect their devices and account credentials.

10. Children's and family use

Run Connect is currently intended for personal and family use and is not designed as a service directed to children. A parent or guardian should supervise use by a minor and consider applicable consent and privacy requirements before enabling location collection.

11. International processing

Cloud service providers may process or store information in countries other than the user's country of residence. Applicable safeguards and provider terms govern such processing.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Run Connect's features and infrastructure evolve. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

13. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, email runconnect@proton.me.
